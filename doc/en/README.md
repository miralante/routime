# Routime

**Web application for occupational therapy activities for people with intellectual disability.**

---

## What is Routime?

Routime is a digital tool designed for people with intellectual disability to practice daily living skills **independently**, without needing a professional by their side at all times.

The app offers **68 activities** organized into **6 modules**. See the full
table of modules and their therapeutic goals in [`SPEC.md`](SPEC.md) §2.1,
and the complete activity-by-activity catalog in
[`activities.md`](activities.md).

---

## Key features

### ✅ Designed for independence

- **Easy Reading**: short sentences, everyday vocabulary, one idea per screen
- **No pressure**: no timers, no negative scores, no punishment
- **Positive reinforcement**: celebrates correct answers with friendly sounds and animations

### ✅ Accessible to everyone

- **Large buttons**: minimum 64×64 pixels
- **Large text**: clear readable font (Atkinson Hyperlegible)
- **High contrast**: light colors on white background
- **Audio only where it adds value**: a 🔊 button appears only when gamification or the activity design requires it (e.g. hearing what is typed on the keyboard, listening to a sequence)

### ✅ In two languages

- 🇪🇸 **Español** (default)
- 🇬🇧 **English** (can be changed from the menu)

## Getting started

### 1. Open the app

Visit **[routime.apptonomia.uk](https://routime.apptonomia.uk)** or open `site/index.html` from a local server.

### 2. Choose an activity

On the main screen you'll see 6 modules. Tap the module you're interested in and then choose an activity.

### 3. Change language

Tap the language button (🇪🇸 or 🇬🇧) at the top of the screen.

---

## Example of use

Imagine you want to practice a board game. In the **🎲 Board games** module, choose **Tic-Tac-Toe**. The app will show the board and let you play against the computer. You can:

1. **Look at** the board
2. **Listen to** the instructions with the 🔊 button
3. **Tap** the square where you want to play

If you get it right, a brief celebration appears. If not, you'll receive an encouraging message: "Almost. Try again!".

## Featured activities

### For working at home

| Activity | What it does |
|----------|-------------|
| **My Routines** | Review daily routines: wake up, get dressed, eat... |
| **The House** | Put in order the steps for household tasks |
| **Emergencies** | Learn when to call 112 and what to say |
| **Shopping** | Practice at the supermarket |

### For thinking and solving

| Activity | What it does |
|----------|-------------|
| **Pairs** | Find the matching cards |
| **Dominoes** | Play a game of dominoes |
| **Tic-Tac-Toe** | Play tic tac toe against the computer |

### For emotions

| Activity | What it does |
|----------|-------------|
| **How do I feel?** | Learn to recognize emotions |
| **Calm** | Guided breathing and relaxation exercises |
| **Among Friends** | Solve situations with friends |
