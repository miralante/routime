# Detailed contents — Routime

> 🌐 **Other language:** [Español](../es/CONTENIDOS.md)

This document is a **detailed didactic index** of Routime. It
expands on [`activities.md`](activities.md) and
[`creating-elements-guide.md`](creating-elements-guide.md) by
giving, for every activity, module and pedagogical concept shipped
with the app:

- Its **name** (as it appears in the UI).
- Its **module** (`tools/` slug).
- Its **didactic objective** (what it works on).
- Its **key vocabulary / theme** (used in `strings.es.js` /
  `strings.en.js`).
- Its **reference** (canonical document and section).

Use this document as the **workbook for the suite**: when a new
activity is proposed, when content is reviewed, or when the catalog
is rebalanced, this is the document to read first.

> **Source of truth for product rules**: [`SPEC.md`](SPEC.md).
> **Source of truth for pedagogy**:
> [`creating-elements-guide.md`](creating-elements-guide.md).
> This document does **not** redefine rules; it indexes the content
> that those rules produce.

## 3. Pedagogical concepts (what each activity works on)

A worked-on skill appears several times across the app. This
section groups activities by the **skill they train**, not by
module, so the team can rebalance the catalog without losing
coverage.

### 3.1 Reading and comprehension

- Reading simple instructions.
- Following short stories.
- Recognising joke structure (Comedy Club).
- Detecting double meanings and figurative language (Idioms,
  Double Meaning).

Activities: `comedy-club/`, `idioms/`, `double-meaning/`,
`dictionary/`, `vocabulary/`, `sentence/`.

### 3.2 Writing

- Spelling (Complete the Word, Colored Spelling).
- Typing (Colored Spelling).
- Building sentences (The Sentence).
- Finding words on a board (Word Search).

Activities: `spelling/`, `colored-spelling/`, `sentence/`,
`dictionary/`.

### 3.3 Number sense and time

- Sequencing events (Times of Day).
- Reading clocks and schedules (My Schedule).
- Counting money (The Shop).

Activities: `times-of-day/`, `my-agenda/`, `shop/`.

### 3.4 Memory and attention

- Visual and auditory memory (Pairs, Echoes).
- Attention to detail (Spot the Difference, What's Missing?).
- Spatial memory (Turns and Mirrors, Where Is It?, The Blocks,
  The Path).

Activities: `pairs/`, `catch/` (sound), `differences/`,
`whats-missing/`, `turns-mirrors/`, `blocks/`, `where-is/`,
`path/`.

### 3.5 Logic and reasoning

- Pattern recognition (Builders).
- Cause/effect and problem-solving (Situations).

Activities: `builders/`, `situations/`.

### 3.6 Social and emotional

- Naming emotions (How do I feel?).
- Reading body signals (My Body Tells Me).
- Regulating frustration (Calm, When It's Hard).
- Social problem-solving (Among Friends, Good Manners, School Rules,
  Circle of Trust).
- Self-acceptance (This Is Me).

Activities: `emotions/`, `calm/`, `resilience/`, `friends/`,
`good-manners/`, `education-norms/`, `trust-circle/`, `self-esteem/`.

### 3.7 Safety and autonomy

- Online safety (Safe Chat, Bullying Chat, Should I Post It?,
  Social media, intimacy and law).
- Street and travel safety (The Street).
- Emergency response (Emergencies, While Help Is on Its Way,
  Before the Emergency, Important Phone Numbers).
- Money autonomy (The Shop, The Shopping).
- Health and self-care (I Tell the Doctor, My First-Aid Kit,
  Healthy Food).
- Personal data (My Details).

Activities: `safe-chat/`, `bullying-chat/`, `post-or-not/`,
`sexual-health/`, `street/`, `emergencies/`, `phone-numbers/`,
`shop/`, `shopping/`, `doctor-visit/`, `first-aid-kit/`,
`healthy-food/`, `my-details/`.

### 3.8 Motor and coordination

- Eye-hand coordination (Catch It, Connect the Dots).
- Fine motor skills (Traces, Coloring, Builders, Piano).
- Spatial motor control (The Theatre).

Activities: `catch/`, `connect-dots/`, `tracing/`, `coloring/`,
`builders/`, `piano-keys/`, `theatre/`.

### 3.9 Body and relationships (with supervision)

- Consent and healthy relationships.
- Body autonomy and privacy.
- Where to ask for help.

Activities: `sexual-health/`, `self-esteem/`, `trust-circle/`.

## 5. Restrictions and forbidden content

The following rules are applied to **every** activity and are
**never** broken (full rationale in [`SPEC.md`](SPEC.md) §3 and
[`creating-elements-guide.md`](creating-elements-guide.md)
§2):

- **No timers, no score, no punishment** — feedback is encouragement,
  not "wrong".
- **No clinical labels** about the user (intellectual disability,
  occupational therapy, minors) inside the UI.
- **No real clinical instructions** in sexual health; the activity
  always routes the user to a trusted person or a health
  professional.
- **No tracking, no login, no analytics** — progress is in
  `localStorage` only.
- **No third-party assets** that could leak behaviour data
  (no Google Fonts CDN, no analytics pixels).
- **No hateful, sexual, political or violent content** in the
  user-facing product.
